How the always-on display works

The clock screen keeps your display lit at low brightness. This uses some battery and, on OLED screens, any static image can contribute to burn-in over long periods. The app offers battery rules, auto-off and burn-in protection; you decide when it runs. Phone makers can change or restrict background behaviour, so the clock may not appear in every situation on every device.
