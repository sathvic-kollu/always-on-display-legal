Privacy policy

Always On Display: AOD Clock for Android · Publisher: Zoopet · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Always On Display: AOD Clock shows a clock, the date, the battery level and, if you choose, notification icons and music controls on a black screen when your phone's display turns off. This policy explains what the app processes, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, and no personal data is sent to us.
  • Your clock faces and settings stay on your phone.
  • Notification access is optional. If you allow it, the app uses only which apps have notifications (their names and icons) and the title of the song that is playing, on your phone, in memory. Message text is never read, stored or sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob (with Unity Ads through AdMob mediation). These partners may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below. A one-time purchase removes all ads.

Information the app processes on your phone

Settings and clock faces

Your chosen face, colours, words for text faces and your rules (charging, battery level, active hours and similar) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Android may include them in your device backup if you use Android backup. We have no access to them.

Device state

To decide when to show the clock, the app reads the battery level and charging state, whether Battery Saver is on, whether the phone is locked, whether a call is ringing or active (from the phone's audio mode — the app does not request the phone-state permission and cannot see who is calling) and, if pocket detection is on, the proximity sensor while the clock is visible. These readings are used immediately and are not stored or transmitted.

Notification access (optional)

If you allow notification access in Android Settings, the app uses:

  • the package name and small icon of apps that currently have notifications, to draw their icons on the always-on screen; and
  • the title and artist of the media that is playing, and play / pause / skip controls, if you switch on music controls.

The app does not read notification titles or message text. This information is kept in memory only while the app is running, is never written to storage, never logged and never sent off your phone. You can turn notification access off at any time in Android Settings.

Display over other apps

This permission is used only to open the clock screen when the display turns off. The app does not draw over other apps and does not read what is on your screen.

Advertising

The app shows banner, native, interstitial and app-open ads through Google AdMob. Unity Ads may serve ads through AdMob mediation. No ads are shown on the always-on screen itself. These partners may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity: Unity privacy policy

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your choices using Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices (when shown for your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play or the Samsung Galaxy Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase with the store on your phone to hide ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it. For data held by advertising partners, see data deletion.

Security

All network connections made by the app and its advertising partners use encrypted HTTPS.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
