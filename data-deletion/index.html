Delete your data

Always On Display: AOD Clock for Android · Publisher: Zoopet · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Always On Display: AOD Clock has no accounts and does not send your settings or notifications to us, so there is no server-side data about you to delete.

Data on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings > Apps > Always On Display: AOD Clock > Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage (or uninstall the app).

This removes your clock faces, settings and the cached Remove Ads state. Your purchase itself stays with your store account and can be restored.

Advertising data

Ad partners may hold data linked to your advertising ID. To delete or reset it, open Android Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads and delete or reset the advertising ID. You can also use Google's and Unity's own controls:

  • Google My Ad Center
  • Unity privacy and data requests
